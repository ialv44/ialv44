## 9 Mothers
**Y Combinator · Spring 2026 · Defense** — A cheap robot that tracks and kills drones flying at sixty miles an hour.

9 Mothers builds counter-drone interceptors: a physically small, relatively affordable robot that detects a hostile drone, tracks it, and takes it down, rather than firing a missile that costs more than the target.

- **Traction.** Around one point six million dollars already booked in sales, with one contract expected to expand to thirty-five million dollars this year, and a claimed pipeline ambition of one billion dollars.
- **Money.** Investors chased it at a valuation upward of two hundred million dollars - the highest-valued company of the batch and one of the highest ever at a Y Combinator Demo Day.
- **Why it matters.** The cost asymmetry of drone warfare is the defining defense problem of the decade, and this is the batch's clearest bet on it.
- **Steal this.** The pitch was not better technology. It was a better unit cost against a threat that is already here.

Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/the-11-standout-startups-from-ycs-demo-day-according-to-vcs/>, <https://mezha.net/eng/bukvy/e82c760a_yc_demo_day/>

## Baseten
**Breakout / benchmark company · YC alum, SPC-backed · AI infrastructure** — The plumbing that serves AI models in production.

Baseten handles deploying, serving and scaling machine learning models, so a company can run inference reliably without building its own infrastructure team.

- **Traction.** Reported total funding of around two point six billion dollars, backed by Greylock, Y Combinator and South Park Commons among others.
- **Money.** Valued at about thirteen billion dollars after a one point five billion dollar Series F led by Altimeter, Conviction and Spark Capital in June 2026.
- **Why it matters.** Inference is where the money in AI actually gets spent day to day, and Baseten sits directly in that flow.
- **Steal this.** A Y Combinator company from 2019 that took six years to become obvious. Timing is a real risk, and patience is a real strategy.

Sources: <https://sacra.com/c/baseten/>, <https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-marketing-robotics-baseten/>

## Ambience Healthcare
**Andreessen Horowitz · a16z-backed · Healthcare AI** — Ambient AI that documents the visit and gets the billing code right.

Ambience listens to clinical encounters and produces documentation, with a heavy focus on coding and compliance - the part that decides whether the hospital actually gets paid correctly for the visit.

- **Traction.** Reported to have raised a two hundred and forty-three million dollar Series C led by Oak HC slash FT, competing directly with Abridge.
- **Money.** Two hundred and forty-three million dollar Series C, reported.
- **Why it matters.** Two very well-funded companies in the same category, differentiating on revenue capture rather than transcription quality.
- **Steal this.** When a category has an obvious leader, the wedge is usually the adjacent workflow the leader treats as secondary.

Sources: <https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/healthcare-ai-funding-deals>, <https://aifundingtracker.com/top-ai-healthcare-startups/>

## Condor Energy
**Y Combinator · W26 · Energy software** — An operating system for how large companies buy and manage electricity.

Condor gives enterprises one system to procure power and optimise how and when they generate and draw it, so electricity is cheaper, cleaner and more reliable.

- **Traction.** Named a Winter 2026 standout by multiple investors.
- **Money.** Y Combinator Winter 2026.
- **Why it matters.** Energy procurement went from a back-office line item to a board-level constraint the moment everyone started training models.
- **Steal this.** When a cost line explodes, the software that manages that line becomes a category overnight.

Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/26/16-of-the-most-interesting-startups-from-yc-w26-demo-day/>

## Etched
**Breakout / benchmark company · Breakout · AI chips** — A chip that does one thing - run transformers - and nothing else.

Etched burns the transformer architecture directly into silicon. By giving up general-purpose flexibility, the bet is dramatically better speed and cost for the one model shape that matters commercially.

- **Traction.** Reported as one of the two largest Series B rounds of 2026 at around five hundred million dollars.
- **Money.** Reported five hundred million dollar Series B.
- **Why it matters.** It is a direct bet that the transformer stays dominant long enough to justify freezing it into hardware.
- **Steal this.** The boldest strategies are subtractive. They removed flexibility on purpose and made that the product.

Sources: <https://www.fastaijobs.com/career-hacks/biggest-ai-funding-rounds-2026>

## Glimpse
**Andreessen Horowitz · YC alum, a16z Series A · Fintech, retail** — Chases the money big retailers quietly deduct from consumer brands.

When a supermarket chain pays a brand less than the invoice - a deduction for a damaged pallet, a late delivery, a promotion - somebody has to dispute it, line by line. Glimpse automates that deduction and dispute process for consumer packaged goods brands.

- **Traction.** A Y Combinator company that pivoted into this, then raised a large Series A on the back of it.
- **Money.** A thirty-five million dollar Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz, with 8VC and Y Combinator participating, announced March 2026.
- **Why it matters.** An unglamorous back-office leak with real money in it, in an industry where the incumbent process is a spreadsheet and a phone call.
- **Steal this.** This is a pivot story. The company that raised the Series A is not the company that got into the batch.

Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/a16z-backed-glimpse-raises-new-funds-accelerates-dispute-tracking-automation-for-cpg-brands/>

## Phyvant
**a16z Speedrun · SR007 · Enterprise AI** — AI that learns how your staff actually get things done, not how the manual says.

Phyvant builds procedural memory for the enterprise - capturing the real sequence of steps employees follow to do a job, so an agent can repeat it rather than improvise from a document.

- **Traction.** Part of the Speedrun SR007 cohort now in progress, with Demo Day on 6 October 2026.
- **Money.** a16z Speedrun SR007.
- **Why it matters.** Same insight as Jedify from a different angle: the missing ingredient for enterprise agents is knowledge that was never written down.
- **Steal this.** Two funded companies attacking enterprise context from opposite ends is a signal, not a coincidence. That is where the market is.

Sources: <https://www.frontrun.vc/blog/a16z-speedrun-cohort-007-companies/>

## Saronic
**Andreessen Horowitz · American Dynamism · Defense** — Autonomous boats, built in America, at a price the navy can buy in numbers.

Saronic builds uncrewed surface vessels - autonomous boats for naval missions, designed to be produced in volume rather than as bespoke million-dollar craft.

- **Traction.** One of the flagship companies of Andreessen Horowitz's American Dynamism practice, alongside Anduril.
- **Money.** Reported to have raised a one hundred and seventy-five million dollar Series B at a one billion dollar valuation, led by Andreessen Horowitz.
- **Why it matters.** Andreessen Horowitz raised about one point two billion dollars for American Dynamism alone in January 2026. Defense manufacturing is now a core venture category, not a curiosity.
- **Steal this.** Same lesson as 9 Mothers, at a bigger scale: in defense, cheap and many beats exquisite and few.

Sources: <https://a16z.com/american-dynamism/>, <https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/a16z-15b-new-funds-american-dynamism-ben-horowitz/>
