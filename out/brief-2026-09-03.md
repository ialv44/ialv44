## Lovable
**Breakout / benchmark company · Breakout · AI app building** — Describe an app in plain language and it builds and hosts it.

Lovable turns a text description into a working web application - front end, back end, deployed - aimed at people who are not engineers. It is the flagship of what people call vibe coding.

- **Traction.** Went from zero to about five hundred million dollars in annualised revenue in roughly fourteen months.
- **Money.** Confirmed a thirteen point three billion dollar valuation in August 2026, raising another four hundred million dollars, up from about six point six billion in December.
- **Why it matters.** It is the clearest evidence that the buyer of software tools is shifting from engineers to everyone else.
- **Steal this.** The market was never engineers who want to code faster. It was the far larger group who could never code at all.

Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/lovable-confirms-new-13-3b-valuation-raises-another-400m/>

## Silmaril
**Y Combinator · 2026 · AI security** — A firewall for AI agents that defends against prompt injection and retrains itself.

Silmaril stops attackers from hijacking an agent through the text it reads - a poisoned email, a booby-trapped document, a malicious web page. Their system detects new attack patterns and retrains its own defenses as attacks evolve.

- **Traction.** Repeatedly named a standout across the 2026 Y Combinator batches, in a year when AI security was one of the densest categories.
- **Money.** Y Combinator, 2026.
- **Why it matters.** The moment agents can take real actions - send money, send email, run code - prompt injection stops being a curiosity and becomes the top enterprise blocker.
- **Steal this.** Every platform shift creates a security category. This is that category for agents, and it is still early.

Sources: <https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/silmaril>, <https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/the-11-standout-startups-from-ycs-demo-day-according-to-vcs/>

## Boardy
**HF0 Residency · HF0 · Consumer AI, networking** — An AI that phones you, learns who you are, and introduces you to people worth meeting.

Boardy is a voice agent you actually talk to. It interviews you about what you are working on and who you need, then makes warm introductions across its network - a super-connector that scales.

- **Traction.** Named among HF0's most notable investments. HF0 writes one million dollar cheques for five percent, the largest single cheque among San Francisco accelerators.
- **Money.** HF0 Residency portfolio.
- **Why it matters.** Voice is the interface where AI feels least like software, and networking is a problem where a machine genuinely has more memory than you do.
- **Steal this.** The product is a phone call. No app, no onboarding, no dashboard. Distribution built into the interface.

Sources: <https://tracxn.com/d/venture-capital/hf0/>, <https://www.hf0.com/>

## Lightsprint
**Y Combinator · Spring 2026 · Developer tools** — Product managers describe a change visually, cloud agents build it, an engineer approves it.

Lightsprint lets a whole team - including non-engineers - plan visually, preview the result live, and ship. You say what you want changed, pick from visual options, an agent builds it, and an engineer reviews and merges the code.

- **Traction.** Named among the buzziest companies of the Spring 2026 batch by multiple investors.
- **Money.** Y Combinator Spring 2026.
- **Why it matters.** It keeps the engineer in the loop as the approver rather than removing them, which is what actually gets adopted inside real companies.
- **Steal this.** The sellable version of 'AI replaces engineers' is 'AI drafts, your engineer approves'. Same output, far less resistance.

Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/18/the-11-standout-startups-from-ycs-demo-day-according-to-vcs/>, <https://yctierlist.com/s26/lightsprint/>

## Profound
**South Park Commons · SPC alum · Marketing analytics** — Tells brands how they show up inside AI answers.

Profound measures and shapes how a brand is represented when people ask AI assistants questions - the search-engine-optimisation problem rebuilt for a world where the answer comes from a model, not a list of links.

- **Traction.** One of the companies most associated with the South Park Commons community, alongside Baseten, Render, Luma and Imbue.
- **Money.** South Park Commons network company.
- **Why it matters.** If people stop clicking links, every marketing budget on earth has to be re-pointed at whatever this category becomes.
- **Steal this.** When a distribution channel changes shape, the measurement layer for the new channel is a company.

Sources: <https://blog.southparkcommons.com/p/spc-founder-fellowship-spring-2026>

## Replit
**Breakout / benchmark company · Breakout · Developer tools** — Build and deploy software from the browser, with an agent doing most of the typing.

Replit is a browser-based environment where you describe what you want and an agent builds, runs and deploys it, with no local setup at all.

- **Traction.** Reported growth from about two and a half million dollars to roughly five hundred and twenty-five million dollars in annualised revenue in around fourteen months.
- **Money.** One of the fastest companies to one hundred million dollars in annual recurring revenue in 2026.
- **Why it matters.** A decade-old developer tool that found a completely new and much larger market by adding an agent to what it already had.
- **Steal this.** Distribution you already own plus a new capability can beat a new company with a better model.

Sources: <https://www.tryanalyze.ai/blog/fastest-growing-ai-companies>
