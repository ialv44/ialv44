## Abridge
**Andreessen Horowitz · a16z-led Series E · Healthcare AI** — Listens to the doctor's appointment and writes the medical note.

Abridge is ambient AI for clinicians. It sits in the room, listens to the conversation between doctor and patient, and produces the clinical documentation, so the doctor is not typing into a computer for hours after every shift.

- **Traction.** The category leader in ambient clinical documentation, deployed across large American health systems.
- **Money.** A three hundred million dollar Series E led by Andreessen Horowitz and Khosla Ventures in April 2026, valuing it at five point three billion dollars, with a reported extension of a further three hundred and sixteen million.
- **Why it matters.** Ambient documentation is the first AI product hospitals bought at scale, and it won because it gave time back rather than replacing anyone.
- **Steal this.** Sell the hour back to the person who hates their paperwork. That buyer signs quickly and stays.

Sources: <https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/abridge-secures-300m-boosts-valuation-53b>, <https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/ambient-ai-startup-abridge-scores-300m-series-e-backed-a16z-and-khosla>

## Mirendil
**Breakout / benchmark company · Breakout · AI research lab** — Autonomous systems that turn compute into scientific discoveries.

Mirendil is a research lab building self-accelerating systems for science - AI that runs the research loop itself across fields like biology and materials science, rather than assisting a human researcher.

- **Traction.** Founded in 2025 by Behnam Neyshabur, Harsh Mehta, Shayan Salehian and Tara Rezaei.
- **Money.** A two hundred million dollar seed round at a one billion dollar valuation, backed by Andreessen Horowitz, Nvidia, Kleiner Perkins and Neo.
- **Why it matters.** A two hundred million dollar seed at a billion-dollar valuation, on a research thesis with no product. That is the extreme end of this market.
- **Steal this.** Reputation is now collateral. A team from the top of the research world can raise a Series B before writing a product.

Sources: <https://www.finsmes.com/2026/06/mirendil-raises-200m-in-seed-funding-at-1-billion-valuation.html>, <https://cryptobriefing.com/mirendil-200m-seed-round-a16z-nvidia/>

## SUN
**a16z Speedrun · SR006 · Consumer AI, audio** — Personalised podcasts and audiobooks you can interrupt and ask questions.

SUN generates audio tailored to you - a podcast or audiobook built for your interests - and lets you talk back to it in real time, asking questions mid-listen and getting answers inside the same stream.

- **Traction.** A three-person team out of Harvard computer science, a Stanford AI PhD, and the founding engineer of Amazon Podcasts. Selected into the a16z Speedrun sixth cohort.
- **Money.** a16z Speedrun invests up to one million dollars, plus up to five million dollars in credits.
- **Why it matters.** This is precisely the format you are listening to right now, built as a company. Personalised, interruptible audio is a real emerging category.
- **Steal this.** Worth watching closely if you ever want to build in this space - they are the closest competitor to your own daily brief.

Sources: <https://genaiassembling.substack.com/p/speedruns-pivot-is-the-playbook-what>

## Sazabi
**Y Combinator · W26 · Developer tools** — Reads your production logs, works out why it broke, and ships the fix.

When something breaks in production, Sazabi analyses the logs to find the cause, then generates the code fix and opens it for you in one click. It closes the loop between the alert and the patch.

- **Traction.** Called out as a Winter 2026 standout by multiple investors.
- **Money.** Y Combinator Winter 2026.
- **Why it matters.** Debugging production is where engineers actually lose their nights. This is the on-call engineer as a product.
- **Steal this.** The winning agent products do not stop at the diagnosis. They deliver the fix.

Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/26/16-of-the-most-interesting-startups-from-yc-w26-demo-day/>

## Superset
**Y Combinator · Spring 2026 · Developer tools** — Mission control for running a hundred coding agents at once.

Superset centrally manages large fleets of coding agents, so a developer can run well over a hundred agent tasks in parallel and still know what each one is doing.

- **Traction.** Named among the standout companies of Spring 2026 by investors.
- **Money.** Y Combinator Spring 2026.
- **Why it matters.** One agent is a toy. A hundred agents is an operations problem, and operations problems are where durable software gets built.
- **Steal this.** Whenever a unit becomes cheap and plentiful, the orchestration layer above it becomes valuable.
